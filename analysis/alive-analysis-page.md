# Open workspaces and sheets from ALive Analysis

The **ALive Analysis** page lists every workspace and sheet you're allowed to run, so you can open any of them in one step.

## Open a workspace or a sheet

1. Choose the search icon, enter **ALive Analysis**, and then choose the related link.
2. Find the workspace or sheet in the list. Workspaces are listed under **Workspaces** and sheets under **Sheets**.
3. Choose the name, or select the line and choose **Open**.

A workspace opens in the workspace viewer and runs at once. If its datasets ask for filters or options, you're asked for them first. A sheet opens on its sheet page.

## What the page shows

| Field | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The name of the workspace or sheet. For a workspace, this is its title in your language when the designer translated it. |
| **Description** | What the workspace or sheet shows. |

The list contains:

- Every workspace, if you have the **ALive Workspaces Admin** permission set.
- Every sheet, if you have the **ALive Sheets Admin** permission set.
- Otherwise, only the workspaces and sheets an administrator shared with one of your permission sets.

A group is left out when you have no permission set for it. For example, if you only have **ALive Workspaces User**, you don't see a **Sheets** group.

## Opened from a card or list

When you choose the **ALive Analysis** action on a card or list, for example on a customer, the page title names the record, such as **ALive Analysis - Customer 10000 · Adatum Corporation**. The page then lists only the workspaces that open from that kind of record, and each one opens filtered to the record. This list appears only when more than one workspace opens from that kind of record. See [Open a workspace from a card or list](workspace-open-in-context.md).

## The list is empty

If the page shows nothing, or doesn't show a workspace you expect:

- Ask your administrator to assign **ALive Workspaces User** (or **ALive Sheets User**) to you.
- Ask your administrator to share the workspace or sheet with a permission set you have. See [Who can see and run workspaces](workspace-permissions.md).
