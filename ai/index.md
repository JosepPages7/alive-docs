# ALive AI

ALive AI is an AI assistant for ALive Analysis workspaces in Business Central. It sends only metadata to the AI provider you choose, never record values.

## Get started

- [ALive AI overview](alive-ai-overview.md): what ALive AI is, what it does for workspace administrators, what it needs, and what it is not for.
- [What ALive AI sends to the AI provider](what-alive-ai-sends.md): what a request contains and never contains, how values are hidden, and where data is stored.
- [Set up ALive AI](set-up-alive-ai.md): step-by-step setup on the ALive AI Setup page, from the Copilot capability to a successful connection test.
- [The provider notice](provider-notice.md): what the provider notice says, when it appears, and how an administrator agrees or withdraws.
